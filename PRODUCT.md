# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Bisma and Rabia at Dot2Shape (@dot2shape.com), an invite-only internal team. They use this portal at their desks during work hours to check project status, ClickUp-driven team workload, and to compile and share weekly status updates with stakeholders. They're already fluent with the tool — this isn't a first-touch audience.

## Product Purpose
Dot2Shape Team Portal is an internal single-page app that centralizes: a live ClickUp-synced dashboard (task status, team workload), a manual projects log, and weekly status reports that can be shared externally via a public, no-login link. Success looks like: the team spends less time manually compiling status updates, and external stakeholders get a clear, presentable snapshot without needing portal access.

## Brand Personality
Polished & precise — clean, confident, no-nonsense. Think Linear/Notion, not a playful consumer app. Navy/indigo glass visual system already established; copy should be direct and functional rather than chatty.

## Anti-references
Avoid the generic bloated-enterprise-software look: dense toolbar clutter, nested cards, gray-on-gray legacy admin panels, over-decorated buttons, invented affordances for standard tasks. When in doubt, match the existing app's own conventions rather than introducing new patterns.

## Design Principles
- Earned familiarity — the tool should disappear into the task, not call attention to itself.
- Restrained color — accent (indigo) is reserved for actions, selection, and state, never decoration.
- One visual vocabulary — same component, same look, everywhere it appears (stat card, form field, badge, etc.)
- Shareable surfaces (the public report view) get slightly more polish than internal-only screens, since external stakeholders see them without context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA: ≥4.5:1 text contrast, full keyboard operability, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives for any animation.
